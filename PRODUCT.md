## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS + Tailwind CSS via CDN, no build step, no backend (fixed by TeoCode's CriadorDeSites.md, confirmed by the user).

## Users

Adults in the Grande ABC / Mauá and Mooca (São Paulo) area, or anywhere in Brazil online, who find Dárquia Moreira through Instagram, Google or Doctoralia and need to decide, on a phone and in a few minutes, whether she is the right psychologist and how to book. Often anxious, tired or hesitant; many are first-time therapy seekers or bariatric-surgery patients. Job: understand what she treats, trust her, and start a WhatsApp conversation.

## Product Purpose

First owned website of psychologist Dárquia Moreira (CRP 06/130418), clinical psychologist specialised in Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (TCC). Success is a visitor tapping a WhatsApp CTA ((11) 98593-9585), tracked via GoatCounter events.

## Positioning

A psychologist with a verified 5,0 rating (57 Google reviews at the Mauá listing, 63 at the São Paulo listing, 71 on Doctoralia) who combines TCC with psychology for bariatric surgery (Protocolo Talita Lopes Marques) and hospital psychology. Few local psychologists cover both.

## Operating Context

In-person care in two offices (Mauá and Mooca) plus online sessions via Google Meet or WhatsApp. Sessions last about 50 minutes. Payment is always upfront (first session by Pix; later by Pix, card or cash). Session prices must not be published (CFP guidance, stated on her current site), so every price question goes to WhatsApp.

- Opening hours are not published anywhere reliable; they stay off the site and go to WhatsApp.
- No logo file exists. The name is set in text.
- Two addresses: R. Campos Sales, 167, Vila Bocaina, Mauá - SP; Av. Radial Leste, 2126, Mooca, São Paulo - SP.
- All CTAs go to WhatsApp with a pre-written message per section. Analytics: GoatCounter only (https://teocode.goatcounter.com/count).

## Brand Commitments

Name "Dárquia Moreira", title "Psicóloga Clínica", CRP 06/130418. Her current site uses cream, black and gold; her photos wear black with a gold necklace. Tone: warm, clear, professional, no hype and no clinical promises.

## Evidence on Hand

- Two professional portraits from her current site (assets/img).

## Product Principles

1. Every claim traces to her own sources; absent facts go to WhatsApp instead of being filled in.
2. One decision per screen on a phone: read a little, trust, tap WhatsApp.
3. Calm over clever: warmth comes from space, her photography and real patient words, not decoration.
4. Accessible by default (WCAG AA, keyboard, reduced motion), because the audience includes people in distress.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, visible focus, semantic landmarks, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, large touch targets. Portuguese (pt-BR) copy in plain language.
