## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm, trust-first one-pager where her face, her real patient words and one WhatsApp action carry everything. Refuses the dark-mesh/purple health-tech landing and the cream-and-brass "wellness" template.
OWN-WORLD: Cool pearl ground (#F6F6F4), graphite ink (#1F2123), one matte-gold accent (#A88646) used for graphics, rules and hover fills, never for body text on light. Outfit display, Figtree body. 16px radius on images and tiles, pill buttons. Bento tiles tinted pearl-dark, one graphite tile. Dark mode mirrors the same tokens.
STORY: Visitor sees a warm professional with a specific specialty, believes it through 5,0 Google reviews in patients' own words, and taps "Agendar sessão" to open WhatsApp with a context-aware message.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 7 cols: two-line H1 "Cuidar da mente começa por uma boa conversa.", one 16-word sub, primary pill CTA plus a text link. Right 5 cols: her portrait on sofa, 4:5, with a solid name/CRP chip overlapping the lower left corner. Nav is one line, 72px, wordmark in text left, CTA right.
FORM: Asymmetric split hero, bento of treatments, featured review plus 2x2, one wide band plus two plan tiles, FAQ accordion, graphite contact band. Form position: user-pinned, seed key: none (pinned).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
